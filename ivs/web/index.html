The model’s own eye — touch it

No dataset: this canvas is the model’s own render, started from noise or, when the source line says birth memory, from the eye the model itself remembers perceiving once. Grab & shake scatters pixels under the pointer (faster shake = stronger). There is no light until you switch it on (Move light or Spotlight left); Reset touch turns it off. The model never sees the numbers behind shift, exposure or light, only the pixels and the rate-limited per-step change as an action. After each tick the canvas is overwritten by the model’s belief, blended between what it perceived and what it expected.

Controls

Intervene: off ignores all touch (shake, light, eye, exposure, wander) so the world model runs on its own; a touch already applied stays until you press Reset touch. Dream: your touch reaches the model only as actions; it perceives its own render, so what you see in the error chart is dissonance (expected vs perceived). Learning (off by default) updates only the dynamics (RSSM): encoder and decoder stay frozen. It learns from that dissonance on what it perceived, and is pulled back toward the frozen copy by pseudo-rehearsal. The frozen copy never changes.
